# Marketing notes: Ads OS

## In one sentence

Your Google and Meta ads on one plain-English screen, with campaigns drafted for you, built switched off and switched on from the app, so nothing spends until you say yes.

## Before and after, for the owner

**Before:** you spend money on Google and Meta, and the figures sit in 2 ad managers that are hard to read. You can't say what an enquiry costs, which campaign is wasting money, or whether this week was better than last. When you want a new campaign you either pay someone, or you stare at a blank screen and hope you've set it up right.

**After:** you open one page and see what you spent, what it brought in and what each enquiry cost, with a green, amber or red light against your own target. Claude writes a few plain sentences on the week and suggests what to change. When you want a new campaign, Claude drafts the whole thing: who sees it, the ads in your voice, a budget inside your limits. You approve it, Claude builds it switched off in your account, and when you're happy you press Switch it on and confirm. You never need to open Ads Manager. Each campaign shows how it's doing against what Claude predicted it would do, and Claude can analyse it and run it again. A Christmas ad can never run in June, and nothing ever spends without you.

Time saved: to check. Do not quote a figure until it has been measured with real users.

## 3 things to show in a 60-second video

1. **One screen, 2 platforms.** Open Overview: spend, enquiries and cost per enquiry against target, with the light, and Google and Meta side by side with the line that says they count a result differently. Click a campaign: its score against what Claude predicted ("Score 112: ahead"), its 12 weeks, and a carousel of every ad in it, each with its own spend and results. Press Analyse campaign and read what is working, what is wasting money and what Claude couldn't see.
2. **A campaign drafted for you, or run again.** Press New campaign, type what it's for, and watch a full draft appear: audience, 3 ads, a daily budget with the "inside your limits" check and the season check. Or press Run campaign on one that worked and answer 5 plain questions: same people or new, same budget or new, the dates, keep the best ads or write fresh ones. Press Approve.
3. **Built switched off, then your click.** Press "Build it in my account, paused" and show that every part is paused. Then press Switch it on, read the pop-up (it says the exact change and the limits check) and press Yes, do it. Claude does that one change and reads it back. Say: nothing spends until you say yes.
