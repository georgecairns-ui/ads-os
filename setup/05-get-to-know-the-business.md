# Step 1 of 7: get to know the business

Goal: a short, accurate note in `context/business.md` so every ad you draft sounds like it belongs to this business. Ask as little as possible. Later steps go deeper on their customer, their budget and their results, so keep this one light.

## 1. Ask, in one message

Say why in one line, then ask up to 3 questions:

> So your ads sound like you, a couple of quick questions. What does the business do, and for whom? What do you spend most of your week on? And have you got a website?

## 2. If they give a website

If you can read web pages, read it first and work out what the business does and who for. Then offer your draft back to them rather than asking again:

> From your website, it looks like [draft]. Is that right, or would you put it differently?

## 3. Fill in the notes

- `context/business.md`: what the business does, who for, website, and anything else they mentioned. Change its status line to `Status: filled in on <date>`.
- `context/priorities.md`: only if they volunteer what matters most right now, without you asking for it separately. Change its status line to `Status: filled in on <date>`.

## 4. Make the app look like their business

Follow `.claude/skills/match-my-brand`: ask for their brand guidelines or website, or 3 quick questions if they have neither, then put their colour, font and logo on the app. If they gave you their website in step 1, say so and offer to use it, so they only have to say yes. Show them the result in light and dark before moving on. If they'd rather keep the standard look, that's fine: skip it.

Tick this step in `setup/progress.md`.
